Welcome to the proceedings of the 1st Workshop on Grounding Language Models:
Learning Faithfully and Efficiently (GroundLM 2026), held in conjunction with
EMNLP 2026.

GroundLM 2026 focuses on methods, benchmarks, and applications for improving
the faithfulness, reliability, and evidence-grounded behavior of language
models. The workshop brings together researchers working on grounded
generation, retrieval, multimodal evidence, scientific reasoning, and related
topics.

The workshop received 147 submissions in total. Among the regular main-paper
submissions, 56 papers were accepted, corresponding to a main-paper acceptance
rate of 38.1\%. In addition, these proceedings include 24 Findings papers and
10 shared-task system reports, which were evaluated through separate tracks.

We thank all authors for their contributions, as well as the reviewers and
area chairs for their careful and constructive feedback. We also thank our
organizing and program committees, invited speakers, panelists, and sponsors,
Tencent and RBC Borealis, for their support.

We hope that these proceedings will encourage further research on building
language models that are not only capable, but also grounded, faithful, and
auditable.
